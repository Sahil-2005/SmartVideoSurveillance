How to use your Android Phone as a CCTV:
  1. Install "IP Webcam" (by Pavel Khlebovich) free from Google Play Store.
  2. Ensure phone and laptop are connected to the same Wi-Fi network.
  3. Open IP Webcam, scroll down and tap "Start server".
  4. Note the IP URL shown on phone (e.g. http://192.168.1.5:8080), enter http://<phone-ip>:8080/video below and test!
For Android IP Webcam, standard URL is http://<phone-ip>:8080/video
